How it works

Applying for a scholarship.

  1. Check eligibility

    Open to students from economically weaker sections across school, pre-university, degree, professional and postgraduate courses. Girl students and students with disabilities are given preference.

  2. Prepare your documents

    Proof of income, marks and admission are required. The exact document list is being finalised with the Scholarship Department.

  3. Submit your application

    Applications are reviewed by the Scholarship Department at the Bengaluru and Delhi offices.

  4. Selection and disbursement

    Awards are decided on merit and need, and paid monthly through the academic year.
